A chemical plant in an urban district has a suspected chlorine leak. The emergency management department issues an official alert and evacuation advice; local media relay and amplify the message; meanwhile, exaggerated or false claims spread on social platforms. Residents in the district exchange information through neighborhood ties and social networks. Each person, based on the messages they receive, how much they trust the authorities, and their own risk preference, decides whether to evacuate immediately, keep waiting, or forward a message. Sometimes officials are slow, sometimes the rumor is loud, and people do not always believe the same thing.
