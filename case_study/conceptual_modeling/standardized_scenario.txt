A ChemicalPlant located in an urban district has a suspected chlorine leak state. The EmergencyManagementDepartment issues an official alert and evacuation advice, where the alert issuance timing may be delayed by a slowness factor. LocalMedia relay and amplify the message from the EmergencyManagementDepartment. SocialPlatform propagates exaggerated or false claims, with a variable volume. Residents in the district are connected through neighborhood ties and social networks. Each Resident has state variables: received_messages, trust_in_official, and risk_preference. Based on these variables, each Resident sets an evacuation_decision state variable to one of evacuate_immediately, keep_waiting, or forward_message. Residents exhibit heterogeneous trust_in_official values, causing differing beliefs in the received messages.
